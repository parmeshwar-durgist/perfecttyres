Patented Innovations That Redefine Mobility

Michelin invests over €600 million annually in tire R&D. Every Michelin radial fitted at Perfect Tyres delivers groundbreaking traction, reduced rolling resistance, and whisper-quiet passenger calm.

EverGrip™ Technology

Self-regenerating tread channels emerge and widen as the tyre naturally wears down, maintaining short wet-braking distances from day one down to the legal 1.6mm tread limit.

MaxTouch Construction™

Engineered carcass footprint evenly distributes acceleration, braking, and cornering g-forces across the entire contact patch, unlocking up to 20% higher usable tread mileage.

Michelin Acoustic™

Custom-engineered polyurethane acoustic foam bonded inside the tyre cavity muffles road resonance and airborne noise by up to 20%, creating silence in luxury sedans and EVs.

Bi-Compound Tread

Dual elastomer compounds across one unified tread: an outer shoulder optimized for crisp lateral dry cornering grip, paired with an inner high-silica compound for water evacuation.

Certified Michelin Mounting & Care

Michelin tires are high-precision instruments. At Perfect Tyres, our technicians utilize Italian robotic equipment to preserve your wheel integrity and maximize radial life.

Touchless Rim Clamping

Corghi leverless tire changers eliminate rim contact entirely, preventing cosmetic scuffs or scratches on diamond-cut and painted alloy wheels.

3D Laser Alignment

High-definition cameras measure camber, caster, and toe to within 0.01 degrees according to official OEM factory database specifications.

Road-Force Balancing

Simulates actual highway weight load to detect radial runout and tyre nonuniformity, completely eradicating high-speed steering vibrations.

99.9% Nitrogen Inflation

Dry nitrogen molecules reduce pressure leakage through porous rubber, preventing internal tire oxidation and keeping operating temperatures lower.
