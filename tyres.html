205 — Nominal Tyre Width (Millimetres)

This 3-digit number indicates the tyre's width from sidewall to sidewall in millimetres when mounted on its recommended rim width. A wider tyre generally provides a larger contact patch and cornering stability.

Placard check: Verify vehicle manufacturer's rim width tolerance before opting for wider tyres.
Where to Check Your Recommended Factory Tyre Size?

Always confirm correct tyre dimensions from your vehicle's driver-side door jamb placard, inside the fuel filler flap, or in the vehicle owner's manual rather than relying solely on existing tyres which might have been altered previously.
