125 YEARS OF AMERICAN SPIRIT

The Firestone Story: Innovation That Moved America

In 1900, Harvey S. Firestone recognized that rubber pneumatic tyres would redefine world transportation. Partnering with Henry Ford to equip the historic Model T, Firestone transformed everyday mobility.

Today, as an integral brand under Bridgestone Corporation, Firestone pairs its historic grit and legendary Indianapolis 500 racing heritage with world-leading Japanese polymer R&D.

Harvey S. Firestone's Creed:
"Best today, still better tomorrow — building quality tyres that stand up to the most demanding roads on Earth."
1900

Founded in Akron, Ohio

Harvey Firestone starts production with 12 employees, establishing what would become America's premier tyre titan.

1911

First Indianapolis 500 Victory

Ray Harroun wins the inaugural Indy 500 on Firestone tyres. Firestone continues to dominate with over 65 race victories at the Brickyard.

1932

World's First Pneumatic Tractor Tyre

Harvey Firestone puts rubber tyres on farm tractors, replacing steel wheels and revolutionizing global agriculture efficiency forever.

1988

Bridgestone & Firestone Fusion

Firestone joins Bridgestone Corporation, creating the world's most capable automotive tyre and rubber engineering powerhouse.

Present Day
