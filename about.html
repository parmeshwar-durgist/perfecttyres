SAFETY & TECHNICAL INTEGRITY

Our Commitment to Accurate Tyre Sizing

Installing an improper tyre width, aspect ratio, or load rating can lead to speedometer distortion, reduced fuel economy, premature suspension wear, and severe handling degradation during emergency manoeuvres.

At Perfect Tyre, our staff follows a disciplined fitment checklist:

1

Manufacturer Placard Cross-Check

We inspect the vehicle driver-side door pillar placard to verify the original equipment (OE) recommended tyre dimensions and cold inflation pressures.

2

Load Index & Speed Symbol Matching

We ensure replacement tyres carry an equal or higher load index and speed rating than the vehicle manufacturer's minimum safety rating.

3

Full Axle Balance Assessment

Whenever replacing two tyres instead of four, we place the newest tyres on the appropriate axle to maintain directional control in adverse weather conditions.
