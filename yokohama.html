Information for Yokohama

Since 1917, Yokohama has defined the cutting edge of tire science. From patented Orange Oil nano-compounds to high-speed Nürburgring endurance testing, discover why Yokohama radials deliver superior safety, mileage, and driving pleasure.

Tokyo Heritage Since 1917

Patented Orange Oil

Proprietary nano-blend technology infuses natural citrus oil with micro-silica, keeping tread blocks flexible for extraordinary wet grip while cutting fuel consumption.

ADVAN Motorsport DNA

Yokohama Technical FAQ

Orange oil is a natural renewable extract that Yokohama blends with natural and micro-silica rubber. It makes the rubber compound significantly more flexible at a microscopic level, allowing the tread to adhere tightly to wet road asphalt while cutting fuel-robbing rolling resistance.
