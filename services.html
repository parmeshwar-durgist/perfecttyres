WORKSHOP PHILOSOPHY

Engineered for Accuracy. Calibrated for Safety.

Proper tyre performance extends beyond rubber compound selection. Even the finest radial tyre will wear unevenly and lose steering responsiveness if wheel alignment angles wander or if high-speed dynamic imbalances are left uncorrected.

At Perfect Tyre, our workshop is configured with computerized wheel service machinery, pneumatic tyre changers, and calibrated torque instruments. We offer comprehensive services designed to ensure your vehicle drives straight, grips confidently, and maximizes every millimetre of tread life.

SERVICE CATALOGUE

Our Tyre & Wheel Services

Every service is conducted according to stringent automotive safety practices.

Tyre Sales & Replacement

Complete dismounting of worn tyres and pneumatic installation of fresh radials. We use touchless bead assist arms to eliminate scratching or rim deformation on alloy wheels, finishing with fresh valve stems.

  • Touchless rim-safe tyre dismounting
  • Bead seating and airtight sealing
  • New valve installation

Computerized 3D Wheel Alignment

High-definition digital camera targets measure wheel orientation relative to the vehicle's geometric thrust line. We adjust Camber, Caster, and Toe angles to stop vehicle pulling and prevent shoulder tread baldness.

  • Restores straight-line tracking
  • Prevents one-sided tyre scrubbing
  • Printout report of before/after angles

Dynamic Wheel Balancing

Radial tyres have subtle microscopic weight variations around the circumference. Our computer spin balancer identifies exact imbalance grams, corrected using corrosion-resistant adhesive or clip counterweights.

  • Eliminates steering wheel wobble at 80+ km/h
  • Protects steering rack & wheel bearings
  • Smooth highway cruising comfort

Tyre Size Identification

Not sure if previous owners fitted the right tyre sizes? Our technicians verify your chassis placard against original equipment factory data to confirm correct width, profile, and speed ratings.

  • Placard vs current tyre comparison
  • Speedometer accuracy check
  • Load index verification

Tubeless Tyre & Puncture Repair

Safe, durable repair of nail punctures occurring in the crown tread area. We perform internal inspection to ensure no internal run-flat casing cord damage before sealing the injury permanently.

  • Internal tyre carcass inspection
  • Permanent vulcanized patch-plug sealing
  • Post-repair submersion bubble test

Tyre Rotation & Inspection

Front and rear tyres absorb distinct braking, steering, and driving loads. Regular rotation every 8,000–10,000 km balances wear patterns, accompanied by digital pressure checks and tread depth measurements.

  • Drive-axle pattern rotation
  • Digital tread depth recording
  • Cold inflation pressure calibration
